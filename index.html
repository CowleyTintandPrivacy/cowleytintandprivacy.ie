Frequently Asked Questions

Everything homeowners need to know before installing window tint and privacy films.

One-way mirror films work by physics: the side with greater light becomes reflective. During the day, the outside is much brighter than inside, giving total external privacy. At night, if your indoor lights are on and it is dark outside, the effect reverses. For rooms requiring complete 24/7 night-time privacy (such as bathrooms or street-level front door glass), we install our translucent frosted matte films which block vision 100% day and night.
Not when the right film is applied. Automotive films or non-breathable dark films can trap excessive thermal heat between double panes, causing glass thermal breakage. At Cowley Tint & Privacy, we exclusively specify European dual-reflective, exterior-rated, or ceramic films engineered to be fully safe for low-E and argon gas double and triple-glazed residential glass units.
A standard residential job (such as a front entryway, patio doors, or 3–6 large living room windows) takes between 2 to 4 hours. The process is completely clean: we lay heavy protective sheets across your floors and furniture, use a gentle slip solution, squeegee off all moisture, and wipe down your sills before leaving.
Our architectural films come with a hard-coat, scratch-resistant barrier. After allowing 30 days for initial curing, you clean them exactly as you would normal glass: using a soft microfiber cloth and a mild soapy water solution or ammonia-free window spray.
