Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Cowley Tint & Privacy ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website cowleytintandprivacy.ie. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our customers, website visitors, and clients in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a quote, or book an installation, we may collect the following details:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, and installation location/eircode.
  • Project Specifications: Details regarding your property, window measurements, tinting preferences, or vehicle specifications.
  • Automated Usage Data: Technical logs, browser type, device information, and IP addresses collected via hosting providers or standard analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing accurate pricing estimates, quotes, and consultations.
  • Scheduling and executing window film installations (residential, commercial, or automotive).
  • Processing customer invoices, receipts, and warranty records.
  • Responding directly to customer service inquiries or support requests.
  • Complying with statutory, legal, and tax obligations in Ireland.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To fulfill a request or contract for window tinting services.
  • Legitimate Interests: To communicate effectively with clients, manage our daily operations, and improve our services.
  • Consent: Where you have opted into marketing communications or non-essential cookies.
  • Legal Obligation: To maintain standard financial, invoice, and tax records.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with trusted service providers necessary for operating our business, such as:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers (e.g., GitHub Pages).
  • Payment and invoicing gateways (e.g., Revolut Pro) to process transactions securely.
  • Communication platforms used to receive contact form submissions or inquiries.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered, including warranty verification and statutory tax record-keeping requirements in Ireland.

6. Your Data Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access the data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • The right to request erasure ("right to be forgotten") under certain conditions.
  • The right to object to or restrict specific forms of data processing.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please reach out via our contact details below.
